3. What information we collect

A. Information you provide

  • Onboarding reflections (e.g. what's holding you back, where you want to grow, how you'd describe yourself, where you want to be)
  • Your professional role category
  • Day 14 blueprint questions (your current role, what you most want to be known for in 12 months, what held you back most at the start, where you felt the biggest change, and what still feels hardest)

  • Your LinkedIn profile content, if you choose to use the LinkedIn audit/rewrite feature — either as pasted text, or as an uploaded screenshot image

B. Voice and practice data

  • Where the programme includes voice-based practice (including High-Stakes Conversation rehearsal), we access your microphone and process the resulting audio to provide coaching feedback (see Section 7).
  • On Day 2, AmplifyU measures vocal pitch, energy, and pauses using on-device audio analysis, and calculates your speaking pace from your transcript's word count and timing. These descriptive scores are sent to Anthropic as text to generate feedback. Day 3 measures only pause timing on-device and sends this as descriptive text to Anthropic. Day 7 measures pitch and pauses on-device, but uses these locally to adjust feedback rather than sending them to Anthropic. None of this identifies who is speaking, produces a voiceprint, or detects emotion (see Section 12).
  • AmplifyU uses your camera in the Day 12 Simulation exercise to record a short self-practice video (60–90 seconds) so you can watch yourself back and improve your body language, eye contact, and presence. This recording stays on your device and is never uploaded, transmitted to any AI provider, or shared.

6. How AmplifyU uses AI

AmplifyU uses third-party AI services to provide certain coaching features. Depending on the feature you use, information you provide may be sent securely to an AI provider to generate a response — such as coaching feedback, a transcription, or a personalised exercise.

Does AmplifyU use your data to train our own AI models? No — AmplifyU does not operate its own AI models; we use third-party providers (Anthropic, OpenAI).

Do our AI providers use your data to train their models? No, not by default. Both Anthropic and OpenAI's standard commercial/API terms state that data submitted via their APIs is not used to train their models, unless a customer explicitly opts into a separate programme to allow this. AmplifyU has not opted into any such programme with either provider. This does not apply to Vercel, which is a hosting/CDN provider, not an AI model provider, and has no equivalent setting.

AI-generated feedback is produced automatically and may not always be accurate or appropriate to your individual circumstances.

7. Voice and audio processing

Where you choose to use a voice-based practice feature, AmplifyU accesses your microphone and processes the resulting audio to provide that feature. Your audio is transmitted to OpenAI's Whisper service (Audio API) to produce a text transcript.

OpenAI's Audio API applies no abuse-monitoring or application-state retention by default, confirmed against their published API documentation — meaning your audio is not retained by OpenAI after the transcript is generated and returned. AmplifyU does not maintain a permanent copy of your raw audio on our own systems. The resulting transcript is used to generate your coaching feedback via Anthropic.

7A. LinkedIn screenshots and image data

If you use the LinkedIn audit feature by uploading a screenshot rather than pasting text, that image may contain your photograph, your full name, your employer, and — depending on what's visible in the screenshot — the names or photos of other people (e.g. connections or recommendations shown on your profile page).

This image is processed by Anthropic's vision-capable model to extract your profile content as text, which then feeds into the same analysis used for pasted text. No OpenAI involvement, no OCR, no separate image-processing provider.

The image itself is not retained by AmplifyU at any point — it exists only in the app's temporary in-memory state on your device and is never written to local storage or any AmplifyU server. It is processed by Anthropic under their standard API terms (see Section 9) and not retained beyond their standard 30-day window.

Because this feature can surface identifying information about people other than you, we'd recommend pasting only the text of your own profile where possible, rather than a full-page screenshot, if you'd prefer not to share this additional visual information.

12. How we protect your information

  • Data in transit between your device and our servers/AI providers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) — TLS 1.2 minimum enforced by default, with TLS 1.3 supported for compatible clients; older insecure versions are not supported, and all HTTP traffic is automatically redirected to HTTPS
  • Access to our hosting and provider accounts (Vercel, Anthropic, OpenAI) is restricted and access-controlled — two-factor authentication (authenticator app) on Vercel; email-based verification code required at login for Anthropic and OpenAI
  • Because AmplifyU stores most of your data locally rather than on our servers, the security of that data also depends on your own device's protections (passcode, biometric lock, disk encryption) — we'd encourage you to use these
  • At-rest encryption: environment variables are encrypted at rest, and file storage/build artifacts are encrypted at rest using AES-256

AmplifyU does not perform facial recognition, biometric identification (i.e. identifying who is speaking or appearing on camera), or emotion detection from your voice or video. AmplifyU does analyse generic vocal delivery qualities on-device on three days, and this varies by day: Day 2 measures pitch, energy, and pauses via on-device audio analysis, plus a speaking-pace score calculated from your transcript's word count and timing — these descriptive scores are sent to Anthropic as text to generate feedback. Day 3 measures only pause timing on-device, sent to Anthropic as descriptive text. Day 7 measures pitch and pauses on-device, but these are used locally to adjust feedback already generated by Anthropic, rather than being sent to Anthropic at all. None of this identifies who is speaking, produces a voiceprint, or detects emotion. Separately, voice recordings used for transcription (see Section 7) produce only a text transcript via OpenAI's Whisper, with no additional analysis requested or performed on that audio. The Day 12 video recording is for your own personal playback and self-review only — no frame is ever extracted from it and it never leaves your device.

Under UK GDPR, voice can become biometric data where it is used for the purpose of uniquely identifying a person. AmplifyU's vocal delivery analysis works differently: it measures how you sound (delivery characteristics such as pitch, energy, pauses, and pace) in order to generate coaching feedback, rather than building a biometric template used to identify who you are. AmplifyU does not process biometric data for the purpose of uniquely identifying users, and does not create voiceprints or identify speakers.
