Cookie Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

Overview

AmplifyU (amplifyu.app) is a static website with no user accounts, forms, or backend — so we don't set any strictly necessary cookies for things like sessions or security. The only cookies in play come from optional analytics, and only after you accept them.

Cookies we use

NamePurposeSet when
_ga, _ga_*, _gidGoogle Analytics (GA4) — helps us understand how the site is used, in aggregateOnly after you click "Accept All"

We also store your cookie preference itself in your browser's local storage (not a cookie), so we don't ask again on every visit.

Managing your preferences

You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie Settings" at the bottom of any page. Choosing "Reject Non-Essential" clears any analytics cookies already set for this domain.

What Google Analytics tells us

When accepted, GA4 helps us understand things like which pages are visited and general usage patterns, so we can improve the site. This data is aggregated and isn't used to identify you personally. For more detail on how Google handles this data, see Google's Privacy Policy.
