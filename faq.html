02 — How It Works

The six-step methodology and AI coaching loop.

Every session follows a six-step coaching journey: 01 Insights - understand the skill and why it matters. 02 Theory - learn the science, frameworks and principles behind it. 03 Example - see the skill in action through real-world examples and communication masters. 04 Rehearse - put the technique into practice through a short, focused exercise. 05 Simulation - use your voice to practise the skill in a realistic scenario and receive AI-powered feedback. 06 Review - reflect on what happened, identify where you can improve, and turn the learning into something you can use in the real world. Learn → Practise → AI Feedback → Improve - that loop is at the heart of AmplifyU.

Because knowing how to communicate and being able to communicate under pressure are two different things. You can understand a framework perfectly and still lose your structure when someone puts you on the spot. AmplifyU gets you out of passive learning and into deliberate practice - helping you rehearse the moments that matter before they happen for real.

Your AI coach analyses your practice and gives you personalised feedback designed to help you understand what you're already doing well and where you can improve. Depending on the exercise, feedback can focus on areas such as clarity, structure, delivery, pace, confidence, storytelling and how effectively you communicate your message. The purpose isn't to give you a score for the sake of a score - it's to answer: "What should I try differently next time?"
